## 4. The 10 weakest points of PRD.md + PLAN.md (booklet Prompt 9)

1. **A requirement that caused a known incident is still in force.** PRD.md:91 – "**Hebrew labels** via `python-bidi`, with numbers and units LTR."
   INC-002 found that python-bidi reverses matplotlib Hebrew twice. The PRD was not updated, and `requirements.txt` still pins the package.
2. **The copy criterion cannot detect the actual risk.** PRD.md:246 (AC-11) – "0 sentences of ≥ 8 consecutive words matching the source papers".
   The sources are English and the book is Hebrew. Translated paraphrase-plagiarism produces zero English 8-grams, so AC-11 always passes. No semantic or translation check is specified.
3. **"Rendered lines" is claimed but not measured.** PRD.md:256 (AC-21) – "exactly one `scenebox` of ≤ 4 rendered lines", and PLAN.md:174 – "`qa-super` counts the rendered lines of each `scenebox`".
   The implementation counts 330 characters of source. Nothing reads the PDF.
4. **"Critically" has no test.** PRD.md:250 (AC-15) – "All issues in §9 are discussed critically in ch. 4, in a `cautionbox`", verified by "`qa-super`".
   qa.py has no AC-15 check, and "critically" has no observable definition (for example: each issue Dn named, number shown, consequence stated).
5. **The PRD claims full recomputation, but it was not done.** PRD.md:261 – "The committee checked Table 1 and Table 2 … and recomputed every value."
   The age χ²(2, N = 1392) = 1.02 does not reproduce (QA-15) and is absent from §9.
6. **A study limitation is understated.** PRD.md:307 – "A single crosswalk in Tel Aviv, one morning hour (07:30–08:30)".
   The paper reports eight one-hour sessions over six weeks. Repeating the PRD wording in ch. 4 would misreport the source in a chapter whose purpose is critical accuracy.
7. **A mandatory graph contradicts its own rule.** PRD.md:130 – "Vector PDF, generated by scripts in `scripts/`, data from cited tables" for a row that includes "**G4** risk heat-map".
   The P and I scores are authored estimates. Either G4 breaks the rule, or the rule needs an exception and a statement in the caption.
8. **Data traceability is asserted but vacuous.** PRD.md:249 (AC-14) – "Every number taken from the flagship paper matches the source; every recomputed number is reproduced by a script".
   `quoted_in_book` is `{}`, and the check is a substring test. The PRD names no mechanism that links a `\num{}` in a chapter to a JSON key.
9. **The page budget leaves no margin.** PRD.md:98 – "**Target: 28–32 physical pages** (minimum 25, per the brief)", with PRD.md:118 – "| 10 | סיכום | 1 |".
   The chapters sum to 26 body pages against a hard floor of 25. Each opener (98 mm band + scenebox) uses about half a page (figures-test p. 1), so ch. 10's "1 page" holds about half a page of content. The ten openers use about 5 of the 26 body pages. One chapter running short breaks AC-02.
10. **The red-team exit criterion can be met by dismissal.** PRD.md:251 (AC-16) – "10 weakest points found in a fresh session; each resolved or answered in writing".
    "Answered" has no acceptance bar, so any point can be closed with a sentence. Nothing defines who accepts an answer or what evidence a resolution needs.
