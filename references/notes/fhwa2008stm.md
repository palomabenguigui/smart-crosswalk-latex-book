## Content used (own words), §5.3 "Phase Intervals and Basic Parameters"
- **Minimum green** guards driver expectancy. If set too low, rear-end crash risk rises. Practitioners use anything from about 2 s to 15 s or more.
- Table 5-3, typical minimum green for driver expectancy:
  - through phase on a major arterial: 10–15 s (speed limit above 40 mph) or 7–15 s (40 mph or less);
  - minor arterial: 4–10 s;
  - collector, local or driveway: 2–10 s;
  - left turn: 2–5 s.
- Where pedestrians cross without a push button, minimum green must also cover WALK plus pedestrian clearance (Eq. 5-1).
- Table 5-4: minimum green for queue clearance depends on the detector set-back.

## Use
- ch. 3 (requirements): the **lower bound on vehicle green** when the adaptive controller shortens pedestrian red.
  Combine with MUTCD §4I.06 (`fhwa2025mutcd`): WALK ≥ 7 s (4 s allowed), clearance at 3.5 ft/s, buffer ≥ 2 s.
- ch. 9: a regulatory and engineering risk ("shortening red below driver-expectancy minimum green").
- A US guideline. Israeli practice must be checked separately.
