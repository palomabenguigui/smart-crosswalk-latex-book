# current2023gtx – Current Lighting Solutions (2023), *GTX City LED Countdown Pedestrian Signals* PS7-CFF1-VLA, spec sheet TRAF289 (Rev. 06/21/23)

**Read:** Full two-page sheet. The product table on p. 2 was checked on the rendered page. GE-branded heritage product line (GE is licensed).

## Specs (pp. 1–2)
- 16 × 18 in module, hand/person overlay plus 2-digit countdown. Outline 17.7 × 16.0 in, depth 13.9 in.
- 120 V 60 Hz (80–135 V). Power: **6 W hand, 6 W person, 8 W countdown**. Start-up under 75 ms. −40 to +74 °C.
- Countdown digits in Portland orange, **at least 9 in high and 7 in wide**, citing MUTCD 2014 §4E.07.
  Symbols and luminance per the ITE PTCSI LED standard (Aug. 2010). Minimum luminance 1400 cd/m² (hand/digit) and 2200 cd/m² (person).
- Controller compatibility NEMA TS-2. **Internal conflict monitor**: WALK and DON'T WALK can never light together, and the default on conflict is hand only.

## Use
- ch. 5: a representative off-the-shelf **clearance countdown head** (the US standard type, matching `fhwa2025mutcd` §4I.04).
- ch. 3: display requirements (digit size, luminance, fail-safe default).
- Note: MUTCD §4I.04 para 09 warns that some countdown logic sits in a timing device separate from the controller, so timing changes need care.
  Our adaptive red changes interval lengths, so this is a timing-integration risk for ch. 9. The sheet itself does not say how this module times its countdown.
