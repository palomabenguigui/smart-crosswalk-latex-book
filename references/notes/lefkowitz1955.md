# lefkowitz1955 – Lefkowitz, Blake & Mouton (1955), J. Abnormal and Social Psychology 51(3), 704–706

**Read:** Abstract (OpenAlex, p. 704). Full text not read.

## Key findings (own words)
- Field experiment at a signalised crossing using a confederate ("model").
- Pedestrians broke the DON'T WALK rule more often when the model crossed against the signal than when the model
  waited or was absent.
- When the model who crossed was dressed as a high-status person, more pedestrians followed than when he looked low-status.
