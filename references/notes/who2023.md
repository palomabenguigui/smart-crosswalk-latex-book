## Facts (own words, from the landing-page overview)
- Yearly road-traffic deaths fell slightly, to about 1.19 million.
- Road crashes remain the top cause of death for children and young people aged 5–29.
- Pedestrians, cyclists and motorcyclists together make up **more than half** of the deaths, mostly in low- and middle-income countries.
- The global target is to at least halve road deaths and injuries by 2030.
