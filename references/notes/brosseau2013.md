## What is verified
- A peer-reviewed case study at signalised intersections in Montréal that examines how **waiting time** and other
  factors relate to dangerous crossings and red-light violations (from the title).

## How the book can use it
- Ch. 2/4: cite only as a study that links **waiting time** to violations. It answers the flagship paper's limitation
  that waiting time was not measured (§4.4 of `rosenbloom2009`) and motivates the curb-sensing component.
