# lipovac2013jte – Lipovac, Vujanić, Marić & Nešić (2013), J. Transportation Engineering 139(2), 165–172

**Read:** Abstract (OpenAlex). This is a different paper from `lipovac2013` (TR-F), by the same team and on a related topic.

## Key findings (own words)
- Video at two closely matched signalised crossings (Serbia; location per the authors' wider work, to confirm), one **with** and one
  **without** a countdown display. Categories: gender, age, individual vs group, slow pedestrians, red-interval and traffic conditions.
- Significantly **fewer red-light violators** at the crossing with the countdown. The reduction was especially marked for women.
- Violations in the **first and last four seconds** of pedestrian red differed significantly between the two crossings.
- The countdown helped pedestrians over 40 and slow pedestrians.

## Use (rejected Alternative 1)
- This is the **best evidence in favour of** a red-phase countdown. The book must present it fairly.
- The A1 rejection then rests on standards (`fhwa2025mutcd` §4I.04), the children's result (`fu2016`), the null result in
  `xing2022`, and the fact that A2 targets the cause (long, lone waits), not only its display.
- It is unclear from the abstract whether the display ran during red, green or both. Check the full text before saying "red countdown".
