# hughes2000 – Hughes, Huang, Zegeer & Cynecki (2000), TRR 1705, 32–39

**Read:** Abstract (OpenAlex, p. 32). Full text not read.

## Key findings (own words)
- Automatic (passive) detectors notice a pedestrian approaching the curb and request the WALK phase with no button press.
  They can also lengthen the clearance time for slow walkers.
- Before/after video at sites in Los Angeles (infrared and microwave), Phoenix (microwave) and Rochester, NY (microwave),
  with detectors added alongside ordinary push buttons.
- After installation, vehicle–pedestrian conflicts fell significantly, and so did the number of people starting to cross on DON'T WALK.
- Microwave and infrared detectors did not differ significantly.
- Field tests in Phoenix showed the detection zone needs fine-tuning to cut false calls and missed pedestrians.
