# who2023israel – WHO (2023), GSRRS 2023 country profile: Israel (1 page)

**Read:** Full PDF (1 page). The numbers were checked against a **rendered image** of the page (INC-000 rule), not only extracted text.

## Facts (p. 1, "Burden" box)
- Population: 8,900,059. High-income country, WHO European Region.
- Reported road deaths: **364 (2021)**. WHO estimate: 375 (95% CI 343–407), i.e. **4.2 per 100,000** (2021).
- Sex split of reported deaths: 77% male, 22% female.
- Split by road user: 37% / 24% / 27% / 6% / 6%. The order follows footnote 1 (four-wheel vehicles, powered 2/3-wheelers,
  **pedestrians**, cyclists, other/unknown), so **pedestrians ≈ 27%** of reported deaths, which is about 98 people
  (our calculation, 0.27 × 364, rounded; label it as derived).
- Israel reports having a national law on pedestrian protection (vehicle standards) and a maximum urban speed limit of 50 km/h.

## How the book can use it
- Ch. 1 (Israeli context) and ch. 2 (problem in numbers): about one in four road deaths in Israel is a pedestrian.
- The male share (77%) fits the flagship paper's gender finding. That is context only, not evidence about red-light crossing.
