# pele2019 – Pelé, Deneubourg & Sueur (2019), Safety 5(4), 79 (open access)

**Read:** Abstract (OpenAlex) and a skim of the arXiv preprint (1805.11828). The MDPI PDF was blocked.

## Key findings (own words)
- Video at signalised crossings in France (Strasbourg) and Japan (Nagoya). Survival analysis of when the **first** pedestrian steps off the kerb.
- The probability of crossing follows three different processes: during red, just before the signal turns green, and after green.
- Country of residence shaped the first mover's decision. The authors interpret this through risk sensitivity and temporal discounting.

## Use
ch. 4 (social influence, first mover): it complements `faria2010` and `zhu2021` (followers). For ch. 7, the moment just before green
is a distinct decision window. Cite at abstract level only.
