# guo2011 – Guo, Gao, Yang & Jiang (2011), Traffic Injury Prevention 12(1), 96–103

**Read:** Abstract (OpenAlex).

## Key findings (own words)
- Waiting durations were filmed at signalised crosswalks in China and analysed with a hazard-based duration model.
  Normal crossings were treated as censored, violations as events.
- Positive duration dependence: the longer someone has waited, the more likely they are to stop waiting soon.
  For most people the tendency to violate **rises with waiting time**.
- About 10% of pedestrians were at high risk of violating early. About half still obeyed after **50 s** of waiting.
- Personal factors (willingness) and environmental factors both mattered.

## Use
ch. 4 (waiting-time theme) and ch. 2: "risk grows with the wait". It supports a cap on red for waiting pedestrians (ch. 3 requirement).
