## Use in the book
- The original statement of **social control (social bond) theory**. People keep to norms because of their ties to others,
  so the presence of others restrains deviant acts.
- `rosenbloom2009` derives its Hypothesis 1 from it (p. 390): a pedestrian alone at the curb should cross on red more
  readily than one standing with others. The Discussion (p. 392) returns to it to explain the result.
- The flagship also cites Hirschi & Gottfredson (1994) and Hirschi (2004) (p. 392). Those are not in our bibliography.
- Ch. 4 (theory): cite as the origin of the social-control argument. Ch. 7: the campaign presents waiting as the group norm
  (figure T4, social-control loop).
