# agd2024agd645 – AGD Systems (2024), *AGD645 Pedestrian Detector* data sheet (Doc. 645DS Iss. 1)

**Read:** Full one-page data sheet from the manufacturer's site; specs checked on the rendered page.

## Specs (p. 1)
- Kerbside pedestrian detector using 3D HD **optical stereo vision**. Detection zone **10 m × 3 m** (polygon mask).
  Mounting height 3–6 m (3.5 m nominal).
- 24 V AC/DC, **8.0 W**. Dual opto output plus IP. Real-time video. Wi-Fi set-up (2412–2472 MHz).
- IP65 and NEMA 250 4X. Operating range −34 to +74 °C. Below **15 lux** an infrared illuminator is recommended.
- Size 181 × 160 × 117 mm, 1.4 kg. Standards include TOPAS 2507A (a UK traffic-equipment specification; reportedly the kerbside-detector one, not opened) and NEMA TS 2 2016.
  A US variant must be special-built.
- The older 2018 brochure (third-party copy) and the TST "iCross" sheet show the same core specs. iCross appears to be a rebadged AGD645:
  the type-approval reference and patent number are the same. Its older figures were −20 to +60 °C and a single opto output.

## Use
- ch. 5: a concrete current product for the **camera/stereo kerbside detector** class.
  The 2018 brochure (third-party copy) describes percentage-occupancy ("volumetrics") data. The 2024 data sheet does not list it, so confirm before relying on it for **counting**.
- ch. 3: realistic targets (zone, lux, power).
- ch. 7: privacy, since the device can stream real-time video. The architecture should process on the edge and send counts only.
