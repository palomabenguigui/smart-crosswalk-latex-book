# Source verification log

Every entry in `refs.bib` must be **verified online** before it is cited (PRD §11, AC-22).
The Researcher opens the DOI or URL and checks title, authors, year, venue and pages against the landing page.
**Unverifiable sources are dropped, never invented.**
